How to display pressed keyboard and mouse keys

Open Blender and click Edit/Preferences. Select Get Extensions on the left and allow access to the online add-on database - Allow Online Access

Then, in the search bar, find Screencast Keys and click Install.

Open the side menu by clicking the small arrow on the right or pressing N on your keyboard.

Select Screencast Keys on the right and tick the first box. Below, you can adjust the display settings – where exactly the mouse and pressed keys will appear.
